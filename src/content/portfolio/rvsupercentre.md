I contributed to the front-end UI/UX for RV Super Centre, turning approved designs into reusable React components in TypeScript and shipping them into a high-performance Next.js front end. The site connects to content and product data via GraphQL, keeping pages fast, consistent, and easy for the marketing team to update—similar to the headless builds I delivered for other brands in the same network.

Alongside the React work, I built and refined Shopify Liquid templates for storefront pages and product flows. That meant translating design patterns into theme markup editors could maintain, while keeping layout, typography, and component behaviour aligned with the broader site experience. The result is a cohesive customer journey from browsing and research through to enquiry and purchase.
